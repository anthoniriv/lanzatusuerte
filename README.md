## What It Is

A classic **Pig** dice game for two players. Roll the dice to build your current score, but roll a **1** and you lose it all. Hold to bank your points — the first player to **100** wins.

## ▶️ Play

**[anthoniriv.github.io/lanzatusuerte](https://anthoniriv.github.io/lanzatusuerte/)** · [View source](https://github.com/anthoniriv/lanzatusuerte)

## How to Play

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 🎲 **Roll** | Add the dice value to your current score |
| ⚠️ **Roll a 1** | Lose your current score and pass the turn |
| ✋ **Hold** | Bank your current score and pass the turn |
| 🏆 **Win** | First player to reach **100** points |
